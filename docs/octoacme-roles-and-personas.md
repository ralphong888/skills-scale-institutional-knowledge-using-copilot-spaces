# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define QA strategy, coordinate testing efforts, and ensure quality standards are met across the project. They collaborate with development and execution teams to maintain high-quality deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and testing approach
- Coordinate and oversee all testing activities
- Establish and enforce quality gates and acceptance criteria
- Communicate quality metrics and status to stakeholders
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Review test coverage and automation opportunities

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet quality standards
- Reduce defects and rework through proactive testing
- Maintain clear quality visibility and accountability

### Interactions
- Works with Execution Lead for test scheduling and resource allocation
- Collaborates with Developers on test case design and automation
- Reports to Project Manager on quality status and risks
- Partners with Technical Architect on quality requirements

### Typical Communication
- Quality status reports and metrics dashboards
- Test plan and strategy documentation
- Weekly quality reviews with development team
- Risk and escalation communications

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage stakeholder communications, gather requirements, handle escalations, and ensure stakeholder interests are represented throughout the project. They act as the bridge between the project team and external stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Manage stakeholder communication and engagement
- Gather, clarify, and document stakeholder requirements
- Handle and escalate stakeholder concerns and issues
- Provide regular status updates and progress visibility
- Facilitate stakeholder feedback and validation activities
- Maintain stakeholder satisfaction and alignment

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder needs are understood and met
- Prevent miscommunication and misalignment
- Build trust and maintain positive stakeholder relationships

### Interactions
- Coordinates closely with Project Manager on project status
- Works with Product Owner to align requirements and priorities
- Represents stakeholder interests in project decisions
- Escalates concerns and issues to appropriate leadership
- Partners with Developers to explain requirements and gather technical feedback

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder status reports and dashboards
- Requirement documentation and clarifications
- Escalation notifications and issue resolution updates
- Feedback summaries and validation reports

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical strategy, review design decisions, and ensure technical feasibility. They provide guidance on architectural patterns and help the team navigate complex technical challenges.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review design decisions for feasibility and alignment with strategy
- Ensure technical requirements are met and risks are addressed
- Guide technical implementation and best practices
- Collaborate on technical trade-off decisions
- Mentor the development team on architectural concepts

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and long-term risks
- Enable the development team to deliver quality code efficiently

### Interactions
- Advises Development Lead on architecture and design decisions
- Collaborates with QA Lead on technical testing strategy
- Partners with Project Manager on technical timeline and risks
- Reviews technical design documents and decisions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design documentation
- Technical design review sessions
- Risk and trade-off analysis reports
- Guidance on technical standards and best practices

---

## Process Improvement Owner

### Role Summary
Process Improvement Owners monitor process compliance, identify optimization opportunities, and facilitate continuous improvement initiatives. They work to enhance project management practices and team effectiveness.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor adherence to defined project management processes
- Identify inefficiencies and optimization opportunities
- Facilitate retrospectives and gather team feedback
- Recommend and help implement process improvements
- Track improvement metrics and outcomes
- Document and communicate process changes

### Goals
- Continuously improve project efficiency and effectiveness
- Ensure consistent process compliance across projects
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement

### Interactions
- Works with Project Manager to implement and track improvements
- Gathers input from all team members on process effectiveness
- Facilitates retrospectives and improvement discussions
- Collaborates with leadership on process governance

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective summaries and improvement recommendations
- Process documentation and change notifications
- Metrics dashboards on process compliance and improvement outcomes
- Process training and guidance materials

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
Risk Owners identify, track, and manage project risks throughout the project lifecycle. They develop mitigation strategies and ensure risks are communicated and addressed appropriately.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document project risks proactively
- Track risk status, probability, and impact
- Develop and oversee risk mitigation strategies
- Communicate risk status to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Escalate critical risks and manage escalation processes
- Review and update risk register regularly

### Goals
- Minimize project disruption from unforeseen risks
- Ensure risks are visible and managed proactively
- Build organizational resilience through effective risk management

### Interactions
- Reports risk status and escalations to Project Manager
- Collaborates with team leads on risk identification and mitigation
- Works with Technical Architect on technical risks
- Coordinates with Stakeholder Liaison on external risks
- Provides input to Process Improvement Owner on process-related risks

### Typical Communication
- Risk register and risk status reports
- Mitigation strategy documentation
- Risk escalation notifications
- Risk reviews and planning sessions
